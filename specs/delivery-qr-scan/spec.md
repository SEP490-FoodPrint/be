# Delivery QR scan for ingredient inspection

## Context

When a supplier vehicle arrives, the school inspection roles (nutrition officer, kitchen staff, ingredient management department, parents committee) need to open the matching delivery quickly. Goods carry QR labels generated by the supplier flow: a public trace link `<PUBLIC_TRACE_ORIGIN>/api/public/delivery-items/<qrToken>` per delivery item; ingredient lots also have their own `qrToken`.

## Requirements

- **FR-001**: `GET /deliveries/inspections/lookup?code=<value>` (permission `delivery.view`, inspection roles only) resolves a scanned value to one delivery of the principal's school and returns `{ id, deliveryNo }`.
- **FR-002**: `code` accepts a trace link (last path segment is used), a raw delivery item QR token, an ingredient lot QR token, or a delivery number. Validation uses `z.strictObject`, trims, requires 1–2048 characters.
- **FR-003**: Scope comes from `AuthPrincipal`; deliveries of other schools are never returned. No match returns 404 "Không tìm thấy xe giao của trường với mã này".
- **FR-004**: Read-only; no schema, migration or response envelope change.
- **FR-005**: The "Giao hàng nguyên liệu" screen offers "Quét mã QR" (camera via `BarcodeDetector`, falling back to `jsqr`) and manual entry; a successful lookup opens that delivery's inspection detail.

## Acceptance

1. Scanning a delivery item label of a delivery in the user's school opens its detail.
2. A label from another school's delivery, or an unknown value, shows "Không tìm thấy…".
3. Roles outside the four inspection roles receive 403.
