# Inspection and retained-sample photos

Every new delivery inspection decision (approval or rejection) and retained sample requires one JPEG or PNG photo, captured on a device or selected from existing files. Save the photo together with the decision or sample, associate it with the responsible record, and permit authorized school users to see it later. Existing records without photos remain readable. Reject missing, invalid or oversized files before uploading or writing records. Preserve the current authorization, transactional state changes and idempotency rules.
